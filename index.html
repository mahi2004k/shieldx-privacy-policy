Privacy Policy for ShieldX

Developer: Tiaf Services  |  Package: in.tiafdigital.shieldx  |  Last Updated: November 27, 2026

Tiaf Services ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ShieldX mobile application (in.tiafdigital.shieldx). This Privacy Policy explains what information ShieldX collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. ShieldX is intended for users aged 18 and over.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account information: When you create an account we store your name, email address and a one-way hash of your password (we never store your password in readable form). You may also add a phone number to your profile. We also keep a user ID, sign-in session tokens and failed-login counters to protect your account.
  • Subscription and billing: Paid Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. We receive your plan, subscription status and purchase verification details from Google Play and keep invoice records (plan, amount, date). We do not collect or store your bank, UPI or card details.
  • Location (optional, only with your consent): If you turn on "Share my location with ShieldX", the app sends your device's coordinates and their accuracy to our server. Sharing is off by default, you can turn it off at any time, and stored locations are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can also delete them immediately from the Account tab ("Delete location data").
  • Spam call blocking: If you choose Android's "Caller ID & spam app" setting for ShieldX, incoming calls are checked on your phone against a built-in list, a list downloaded from our server, and numbers you add yourself. We do not access or upload your call history or contacts. Only if you choose to report a number does ShieldX send that number and a category to our server; a reported number is added to the shared list only after several different users report it.
  • Link scanner: A link you submit to the link scanner is sent to our server to be checked against our blocklists and is not stored.
  • Wi-Fi security check: To assess a network, ShieldX may send its name, encryption type and signal strength to our server for analysis. This information is not stored.
  • Protection settings and activity records: We store your protection on/off setting, privacy and consent choices, in-app notifications, and records of when you connected the protection feature and which location you picked.
  • On-device link protection (Android VpnService): Real-time link protection runs only on your device and filters DNS lookups against malware, ad and tracker lists. Your browsing traffic is not routed through ShieldX servers and we do not log or sell your browsing history.
  • On-device checks and Secure Vault: App risk scan, device security checks and the Password Vault run on your phone. Vault entries are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using your master password, which is never stored or sent anywhere. Vault data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Breached-password check (optional): If you tap "Check for breached passwords", ShieldX sends only the first 5 characters of a one-way hash of each password to the Pwned Passwords service. Your passwords themselves are never sent.

2. App Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to reach our servers and check connection security.
  • Wi-Fi state and location: to read the connected Wi-Fi network for the Wi-Fi security check, and for optional location sharing.
  • Biometric: to unlock the app or vault with fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by Android and never reaches ShieldX.
  • VPN permission: to run the on-device link protection.
  • Run at start-up: to restart protection after your phone reboots.
  • Caller ID & spam app role (optional): to let ShieldX screen incoming calls on your phone.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain ShieldX security features and your account.
  • To verify subscriptions and unlock Pro features.
  • To keep the service and its users safe, including preventing fraud, abuse and false spam reports.
  • To respond to support, data access and deletion requests, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the app: Google Play (subscription billing), our cloud hosting provider, and the Pwned Passwords service for the optional breached-password check (hash prefix only). We may also disclose information when required by law. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our servers may be located outside your country.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Account and subscription records are kept while your account is active.
  • Location data is deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier when you delete it.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Account tab → Delete account, confirmed with your password). This permanently erases your account and the server-side data linked to it.
  • If you cannot access the app, see our account deletion page or email us.
  • Records we are legally required to keep (for example for tax) are kept only for the required period. Billing records held by Google Play follow Google's own policies.

6. Your Choices and Rights

In the app you can download a copy of your data, turn location sharing on or off, delete your stored location data and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR and UK GDPR), you may also have the right to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, object to processing and complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights or raise a concern, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We protect data with HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords and account lockout after repeated failed sign-ins. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and master password.

8. Children's Privacy

ShieldX is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date, and we will notify you in the app if a change is significant.
