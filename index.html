Privacy Policy for ShieldX

Developer: Tiaf Services  |  Package: in.tiafdigital.shieldx  |  Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Tiaf Services ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ShieldX mobile application (in.tiafdigital.shieldx). This Privacy Policy explains how ShieldX collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account & Authentication Information: If you create or sign in to a ShieldX account, we process basic account details (such as your email address and authentication credentials) to verify your identity and sync your account status.
  • Subscription & Billing Data: Paid subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We receive subscription status and purchase verification tokens from Google Play to unlock Pro features. We do not collect or store your bank account, UPI, or card details.
  • On-Device Link Protection (Android VpnService): ShieldX's real-time link protection uses Android's local VpnService strictly on your device to filter DNS domain lookups against known malware, ad, and tracking lists. Your browsing traffic is not routed through external ShieldX VPN servers, nor do we log or sell your browsing history.
  • On-Device Security Checks & Secure Vault: Features such as App Risk Scan, Wi-Fi Safety Check, Device Security Checklist, and Secure Vault inspect installed app permissions, network security status, and device settings locally on your phone. Vault entries are encrypted/stored locally on your device and protected by your device screen lock or biometric authentication.

2. How We Use Information

We use processed information solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve ShieldX security features.
  • Authenticate user accounts and verify Google Play subscription entitlements.
  • Respond to customer support and data deletion requests.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We only share necessary verification data with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the app (such as Google Play Billing for subscription validation and our cloud backend hosting), transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and subscription verification records are retained only for as long as your account remains active or as required to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated server data at any time by emailing us at servicestiaf@gmail.com. Local vault and app data can be erased anytime from your phone's settings or by uninstalling ShieldX.

5. Children's Privacy

ShieldX is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
