Delete Your ShieldX Account

App: ShieldX (in.tiafdigital.shieldx)  |  Developer: Tiaf Services

You can permanently delete your ShieldX account and associated personal data at any time either directly inside the ShieldX app or by submitting a web request below.

Option 1: Delete Instantly Inside the ShieldX App

  1. Open the ShieldX app on your Android device and sign in.
  2. Go to Account / Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request to permanently delete your account immediately.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion via Web / Email

If you have already uninstalled ShieldX or cannot access the app, send an account deletion request from your registered email address to:

Email: servicestiaf@gmail.com
Subject: ShieldX Account Deletion Request
Include: The email address linked to your ShieldX account.

Web and email deletion requests are verified and completed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days), and you will receive a confirmation email once completed.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Your ShieldX account credentials, profile email address, user ID, and associated server-side account records are permanently erased. Any local vault data stored on your device is cleared when you uninstall the app or clear app storage.
  • Retained for Legal/Tax Compliance: Minimal anonymized payment transaction references required by applicable tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention laws may be retained for the legally mandated retention period.
  • Active Subscriptions: Deleting your ShieldX account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Please cancel any active subscription in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
