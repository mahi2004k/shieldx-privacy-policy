Delete Your ShieldX Account

App: ShieldX (in.tiafdigital.shieldx)  |  Developer: Tiaf Services  |  Last Updated: November 27, 2026

You can permanently delete your ShieldX account and the personal data linked to it at any time, either directly inside the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1: Delete Instantly Inside the App

  1. Open the ShieldX app on your Android device and sign in.
  2. Open the Account tab and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you have uninstalled ShieldX or cannot sign in, email us from the address registered to your account:

Email: servicestiaf@gmail.com
Subject: ShieldX Account Deletion Request
Include: The email address linked to your ShieldX account.

We verify and complete email requests within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days) and send you a confirmation once they are done.

Delete Only Your Location Data

If you only want to remove location data, open the Account tab and tap Delete location data. You can also turn off "Share my location with ShieldX" there. Stored locations are deleted automatically after 30 days in any case.

What Is Deleted and What Is Retained

  • Permanently deleted: your account, sign-in sessions, name, email address, phone number, user ID, shared location data, notifications, protection settings and activity records, subscription and invoice records held on our servers, and any spam-number reports linked to your account. Vault data and app data stored on your phone are removed when you uninstall ShieldX or clear its storage, because they are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Retained where the law requires: records we must keep for tax, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons are kept only for the legally required period. Billing records held by Google Play follow Google's own policies.
  • Active subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions to avoid further charges.
